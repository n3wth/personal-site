# Oliver Newth (n3wth.com)

> Oliver Newth is an AI Product Lead at Google (2025 to present), developing AI platforms that integrate DeepMind models into production software. Previously at Covariant, Meta, and Microsoft. MEng from MIT (Kennedy Scholar). His independent AI projects are separate from his work at Google. He also builds large-scale light installations for Burning Man and San Francisco memorials.

## Projects and tools

Independent projects, tools, and experiments:

- [Elephant-Goldfish Model (EGM)](https://n3wth.com/projects/elephant-goldfish): Google's open-source plugin for designing and reviewing software with coding agents. A deep-context agent drafts the specification and fresh zero-context reviewers test it before code is written. Oliver was part of the team that built and released it. Dave Rensin introduced the model. Source: https://github.com/google/elephant-goldfish-model
- [markup](https://github.com/n3wth/markup): An independent prototype exploring personal AI agents in shared documents and chat, with visible cursors and edits.
- [hop.flights](https://hop.flights/): Points-vs-cash flight optimizer — finds the cheapest way to fly, miles or money.
- [lunchmoney.sh](https://lunchmoney.sh/): Unofficial Lunch Money plugin for Claude, Codex, and Cursor. Read-only spending, budgets, and bills.
- [r3](https://n3wth.com/projects/r3): Memory for AI apps using vector search and knowledge graphs.
- [@n3wth/ui](https://n3wth.com/projects/ui): Shared React components, theme tokens, and page compositions.
